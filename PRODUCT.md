## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user. The planning recommendation is a static Astro website with TypeScript, an isolated React/Three.js showroom, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and a small carousel controller. This is a proposed build choice, not an existing implementation.

## Product Purpose

A promotional landing website for Mac2Din2 House of Curtains. The primary outcome is that a visitor initiates contact with the business.

## Users

Planning assumption: homeowners comparing curtains and blinds for their rooms. Interior designers are a secondary audience; neither audience was explicitly identified by the user.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- An immersive curtain showroom with substantial 3D, scroll-controlled animation, and parallax.
- Carousels showcasing the business's actual installations.
- The actual business logo, prepared at appropriate quality for website use.
- Direct contact routes rather than a purchasing workflow.
- The user delegated remaining creative and technical choices.
- Current requested deliverable: planning. A production website has not been requested or built in this conversation.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing house/MD monogram, gold and dark roof, brand lettering, and proportions. The logo reads “Mac2Din2 House of Curtains”; the Instagram display name uses “MAC2DIN2 HOUSE OF CURTAIN.” Use the logo wording in editorial website copy.
