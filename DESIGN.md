# Mac2Din2 showroom design

Authority: the original approved showroom design plus the user's November 2 revisions in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-showroom-revision-design.md`.

- Palette comes from the actual logo: warm white #faf7ef, charcoal #26231e, antique gold #9c752d, champagne #d8c69d. Olive removed from the interface and fabric selector.
- Bodoni Moda for editorial headings; Manrope for controls and body. Local fonts. Contact remains primary, with actual phone and Instagram routes.
- One persistent illustrative 3D window scene spans the entire page. Continuous camera position follows document progress; chapters, text and media move at independent depths. Gallery surfaces fade into the common scene instead of cutting to a replacement background.
- A lightweight GPU curtain mesh gathers at the rail, with a delayed lower hem and damped sway. This deterministic cloth response avoids running a physics solver or loading the room during the entrance. Skip, timeout, no-JS and reduced-motion paths remain.
- A detailed framed window sits behind the sheers, with mullions, handles, glazing, sill and an illustrative outdoor garden landscape without houses. The room is illustrative, galleries are actual installations.
- 13 photographs and 3 films from the user's two media sets replace all old public photography. Two photo carousels, three independent film cards, one distinct feature photo. Consistent ratios per gallery, continuous touch/arrow/keyboard navigation. Loop sentinels are inert duplicates for transitions only.
- Films are silent, inline, lazily attached and autoplay only while visible. Manual pause persists through viewport changes. Reduced motion/Data Saver use manual play.
- Responsive layout, static HTML content and contact routes remain usable when motion/WebGL cannot run. User source originals remain untouched in Downloads.
